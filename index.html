Research

Four connected questions, one goal: biosignals clean enough to trust in daily life.

Motion-artifact removal

Denoising PPG and ECG while preserving beat timing, HRV and waveform morphology.

Signal quality assessment

Gates that decide when a segment is trustworthy, so models fall back gracefully instead of guessing.

Wearable cardiovascular monitoring

Heart-rate estimation, arrhythmia classification and non-invasive glucose screening.

Transformers and digital twins

Motion-conditioned attention today. Physiological simulators for stress-testing models are an emerging direction.
